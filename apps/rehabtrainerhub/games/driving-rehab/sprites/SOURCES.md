# Sprite sources

`driving-sprites.png` and `driving-sprites.json` are generated by
`scripts/driving-pixel-sprites/build.mjs`, which renders 3D models from the
kits below into small pixel sprites (vehicles from five angles, props,
facades and the skyline) and packs them into one atlas.

| Kit | Version | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Car Kit | 3.1 | Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit | CC0 1.0 |
| City Kit (Commercial) | 2.1 | Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial | CC0 1.0 |
| City Kit (Roads) | 2.2 | Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads | CC0 1.0 |
| Nature Kit | 2.1 | Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit | CC0 1.0 |
| Mini Characters | 1.0 | Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters | CC0 1.0 |

Each kit's `License.txt` states "Creative Commons Zero, CC0". The kits are not
stored in this repository; download them from the links above and pass the
folder to the build script with `--kits <dir>`.

Two parts are drawn by the build script itself and released under CC0 1.0:
the scooter body (simple box geometry, ridden by a Mini Characters figure)
and the traffic-light lamp discs placed on the City Kit (Roads) signal housing.

The cockpit, road surface, markings, direction signs and finish flag are drawn
in code at run time. The steering wheel image comes from
`../textures/steering-wheel.svg`, listed in `../textures/SOURCES.md` (CC0).
